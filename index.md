# My Omrah Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

My Omrah (Android package: com.lotfi.myomrah) is developed by Lotfi Boucherit. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Information stored on your device

My Omrah stores your Tawaf and Saee counts, session dates, lap start and finish times, lap durations, and saved Omrah history locally in the app's private storage. It uses these records only to show your progress and history. Your chosen app language is also saved locally on your device. Timing also uses the device clock and a device boot counter to calculate durations; this information is not transmitted.

## No collection or sharing by the developer

The app does not send your records to the developer or any server. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics, third-party tracking SDKs, or Internet permission. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files outside its private storage. The app disables Android's automatic backup of its data.

## Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Starting a new Omrah resets the current counters but keeps saved history. To delete all records, use Android Settings > Apps > My Omrah > Storage > Clear storage (wording may vary by device). There is no developer-held copy to delete or restore.

## Contacting the developer

For privacy questions, contact Lotfi Boucherit at lotfi.boucherit@gmail.com. If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or your worship history unless necessary. Your email provider and the developer's email provider process that correspondence under their own policies.

## Children

The app has no age-based account or tracking features and does not collect children's information through the app.

## This website and distribution platforms

This policy describes the Android app. If you visit this policy on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes website requests under its own privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Google Play and Android may separately process information under their own policies; those services are not operated by the developer.

## Changes

If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated with a new revision date. You can read the included policy offline from the app's About button.
